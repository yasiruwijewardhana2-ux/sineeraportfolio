About the Photographer

Fifteen Years
Behind the Frame

I started SINEERA on a simple belief: a photograph should hold the feeling of a moment, not just its likeness. That means shooting for light before anything else, waiting for the unguarded second, and editing with restraint.

Since 2011 I've photographed weddings on three continents, shot campaigns for independent fashion labels, and built a small team that shares the same obsession with story over spectacle. Every project — big or small — gets the same patience.
